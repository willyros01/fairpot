Fairpot Privacy Policy

Effective September 25, 2026

Fairpot does not collect any personal information. There is no account, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Nothing you enter is sent to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

  • Your expenses, receipt amounts and categories.
  • Receipt photos you choose to attach.
  • Your payment details (for example an e-Transfer email or GCash number) and settings.

All of this is stored only in Fairpot’s private storage on your device. If your device’s own backup (such as iCloud Backup) is turned on, it is included in that backup, which is controlled by you and your device maker, not by Fairpot.

Exchange rates

Once a day Fairpot downloads a public table of currency exchange rates from ExchangeRate-API. The request contains no personal information. As with any internet request, that service can see your device’s IP address; its own privacy policy applies to it.

Sharing and backups

When you share a split or save a backup, Fairpot hands the text, photos or backup file to your device’s share sheet. Where it goes next — a message, an email, your Files app — is your choice, and that app’s privacy policy applies.

Camera and photo library

Fairpot asks for access to your camera and photo library only so you can attach a receipt photo. It never reads other photos.

Children

Fairpot collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
